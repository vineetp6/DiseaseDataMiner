Analysis Information

About Analysis Options

Select an analysis type from the options panel to analyze this compound. Three types of analysis are available:

Molecular Properties

Calculates basic molecular properties such as molecular weight, LogP, hydrogen bond donors/acceptors, and other physicochemical properties important for drug discovery.

Binding Affinity Prediction

Uses machine learning to predict the binding affinity of the compound to specific disease targets. This helps identify compounds with potential therapeutic activity against neglected disease targets.

ADMET Properties Prediction

Predicts Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion, and Toxicity properties of the compound. These properties are crucial for determining drug-likeness and potential issues in drug development.
