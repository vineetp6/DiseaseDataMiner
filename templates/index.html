About this Platform

What are Neglected Diseases?

Neglected tropical diseases (NTDs) are a diverse group of conditions that affect more than a billion people worldwide, primarily in low-income populations in tropical and subtropical regions. Despite their significant impact, these diseases often receive limited attention and research funding compared to other health issues.

Our Approach

This platform leverages advanced computational methods and artificial intelligence to accelerate the discovery of potential drug candidates for neglected diseases. Our approach includes:

  • Machine learning models to predict binding affinity to disease targets
  • ADMET property prediction to assess drug-likeness
  • Molecular visualization for structure-based analysis
  • Integration of biological and chemical data

How to Use this Platform

Researchers can use this platform to:

  1. Search for compounds by chemical structure, properties, or disease target
  2. Identify and validate potential drug targets for specific diseases
  3. Screen and rank compounds based on binding affinity and other properties
  4. Analyze compounds for their potential effectiveness against neglected diseases
  5. Visualize molecular structures in both 2D and 3D representations
  6. Access predictive analytics through the interactive dashboard
  7. Evaluate ADMET properties and binding affinities through AI-driven predictions
